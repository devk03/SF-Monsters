# Original coastal terrain atlas

Built-in imagegen. Style inputs: original SF rowhouse and street atlases in this repository; no commercial-game art used as an input.

Create an original top-down GBA-era pixel-art terrain tilesheet for San Francisco's Outer Sunset and Ocean Beach. Supporting STYLE references: our own coral/teal rowhouse and our own gray street atlas; do not copy their shapes into the terrain. Exactly 4 columns x 4 rows of equal square tiles, no gaps, gutters, labels, outlines around cells or extra margins. Every tile represents exactly 16x16 solid native pixel blocks, enlarged uniformly on an aligned grid. Very limited shared palette: muted sage/olive grasses, warm dune sand, Pacific navy/teal water with cream foam, charcoal and silver railings. Every cell is opaque, including background ground. Crisp flat pixel blocks, no gradients, noise, blur, antialiasing, detailed concept art, logos, people or text. Each ground/water texture must tile seamlessly when repeated. Ordered cells, read left to right:
Row 1: (1) short coastal lawn, sparse tiny two-pixel blades over sage ground; (2) warm Ocean Beach dune sand with sparse short darker sand marks; (3) Pacific ocean ripple animation A, dark teal water with horizontal short highlights; (4) north-south shoreline animation A, dark teal water on LEFT, narrow foamy surf strip along center, warm dune sand on RIGHT.
Row 2: (5) tall encounter dune grass, chunky green tufts with dark readable bases over sage ground, filling the lower half without blocking readability; (6) horizontal low charcoal metal pedestrian railing on short lawn, connects left/right edges, one-pixel posts and readable gaps; (7) same railing vertical, connecting top/bottom edges; (8) same railing northwest corner, connects RIGHT and BOTTOM edges.
Row 3: (9) blank cream rectangular neighborhood sign on one charcoal post over lawn, no text or marks; (10) squat gray concrete bollard over sage lawn, softly angled top and dark base; (11) Pacific ripple animation B matching cell 3 exactly in palette and scale, highlights advanced slightly horizontally; (12) shoreline animation B matching cell 4, foamy wave slightly advanced toward sand, same sand edge alignment.
Row 4: (13) subtle alternate short lawn texture, same sage base and density as cell 1; (14) another alternate short lawn texture, same sage base and density as cell 1; (15) Pacific ripple animation C, highlights advanced another small step, loops smoothly back to A; (16) shoreline animation C, foam recedes slightly, loops smoothly back to A.
Keep rails and sign proportions true to 16x16 terrain. Clear native-sized pixel silhouettes. This is production bitmap art for an original SF RPG, without any commercial-game artwork.

Technical conversion: four equal rows/columns, nearest 16x16 native cells, one 15-color RGB555 palette plus transparent index zero, three native wave stages. Human quality approval remains pending.
