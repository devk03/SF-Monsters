Use case: stylized-concept
Asset type: ORIGINAL connected San Francisco street-block background sprite for a GBA top-down RPG. Production artwork candidate, not a screenshot, no characters, no interface.
Primary request: design ONE cohesive row of four attached San Francisco buildings WITH their own roof planes, foundations, recessed entrances, tiny entrance steps and immediate ground contact, drawn as a single coherent architectural block. It must feel like a unified 2003-era 16-bit handheld RPG environment at a native 176 x 80 pixel canvas, not detailed frontal illustrations pasted on paving. Render as clean magnified pixel art with large intentional pixel clusters.
Composition: wide 2.2:1 artwork footprint, tightly framed, transparent beyond the footprint. Left to right widths proportional to 48,32,48,48 native pixels: a muted coral bay-window house; a narrow brick coffee storefront; a butter-cream bay-window house; a muted lilac bay-window house. They share side walls and roof-to-wall modules, touch directly with NO gaps or isolated sticker borders.
Projection and scale: orthographic RPG overhead three-quarter view, observer looks DOWN from the south. Substantial visible gray slate roof planes occupy about the TOP THIRD of the blocked 64-pixel building depth, with consistent depth/foreshortening across all four buildings. Front wall faces are shorter and simpler; recognizable bay windows are geometric projections with clearly lit left/top faces and shaded right faces. No realistic perspective convergence.
Door scale: each entry approximately 10-12 native pixels wide relative to a 16-pixel-wide player. Doors and windows must share a consistent pixel module. No huge garage doors. Cafe has one broad dark window plus a single recessed door. Restrained warm cream trim, dark charcoal blue windows, no glass sparkle noise.
Foundation and walkable bottom strip: the first 64 native rows hold roof and wall. The bottom 16 native rows are the IMMEDIATE approach to these buildings: each wall terminates in a broad subtle 2-pixel foundation mass, doors connect to compact 1- or 2-step stone entry landings, tiny planted recesses can nest beside walls. Ground contact is attached directly to the walls. Quiet neutral light-gray approach paving, matched shadow direction from upper left. Do not draw a continuous skinny dark horizontal rail/fence across the entire frontage. No posts, poles, metal rails or detached long bars. Keep the lowest 8 rows quiet and walkable.
Style: authentic deliberately low-resolution pixel art, hard pixel edges, no gradients, no antialiasing, no painterly texture, no fine realistic micro-detail. About 24 restrained colors total. Broad coherent material clusters, common outline thickness, roofs simpler than wall details. Beautiful warm daylight with neutral gray roofs and sidewalk; not gloomy, yellow paving or teal.
Constraints: original architecture and art only; do not copy any commercial game sprites. No brands, text, people, vehicles, UI, borders, labels or watermark. No large outer floating shadow. Entire connected block visible, roofs uncut, no margins between buildings. Alpha transparency outside the block. This is one continuous complete block, not four separated sprite cards.
