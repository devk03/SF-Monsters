Original Steven Hao battle-portrait candidate for the existing Cognition trial.

Built-in image generation uses our original field atlas for the established
fictional appearance and our original Scott portrait for rendering conventions.
Dark rectangular glasses, gray shirt/navy collar, navy trousers and dark shoes
carry the field costume into battle. Notebook, pose and game conduct are invented.

prompt.md preserves the exact generation request. source.png is the selected
original output. palette-policy.json separates light shirt, navy cloth, warm skin,
dark hair/glasses and white lens openings in sixteen native colors. Conversion
produces one 64x64 four-bit frame, 2048 packed bytes, a 32-byte palette and a
transparent preview. The visible figure uses a 58-pixel height/baseline62.

No photograph or commercial-game sprite is an art input. Human likeness,
pixel-art and battle-presentation approval are pending.
