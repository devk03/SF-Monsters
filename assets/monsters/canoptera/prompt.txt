Use case: stylized-concept. Original GBA creature RPG sprite atlas for SF Mini Monsters. CANOPTERA is the final evolution of a lime/olive garden slug with cream-tipped eyestalks, a fern-leaf mantle, curled tail and a small white blossom. It finally becomes a gentle leaf-winged garden MOTH: keep a small soft green abdomen, two expressive cream-tipped stalk-like antennae, dark friendly eyes, pale cream chest and white blossom at the collar. Broad veined green wings resemble unfurled fern leaves, with rounded natural lobes and cream highlights, not ordinary butterfly patterns. The body remains recognizably related to the slug; original fantasy creature, not an existing Pokemon or branded design, no humanoid arms or clothes. Early-2000s GBA monster pixel art: crisp square clusters, dark one-pixel outlines, restrained green/cream three-tone ramps, upper-left light, 15 opaque colors plus transparency. Enlarge logical pixel art with nearest-neighbor blocks, no painterly gradients. Transparent 1536x1024 atlas in three columns. Upper y=0..700: three 512x700 panels, full creature with wing tips inside each panel and generous clear margins, same scale and shared ground line near y=630. Left enemy front three-quarter facing bottom-left, wings open. Middle same front with subtle wing/antenna motion for entrance animation. Right separate genuine rear three-quarter facing top-right, back of head and veined wing surfaces visible, never flipped front. Lower y=700..1024: left and middle contain separately designed simplified 24x24 logical-pixel party icons with bold wing/body/antenna silhouettes and an alternate wing pose, not merely shrunken battle art. Lower-right completely blank transparent. No background, glow, scenery, shadows, labels or cell borders. All wing tips and antennae must fit fully inside their panels.
