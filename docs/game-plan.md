# Game plan

The consolidated [v1 specification](game-spec.md) replaces the earlier concept plan.

It includes the 16-neighborhood campaign, eight proposed real-person gym leaders,
150 mini monsters, adult comedy systems, championship, platforms, open-source
release, and a subsequent expansion of approximately 50 researched Twitter heads.

[Cast research](cast-research.md) records public sources and casting proposals.
Specific names, elemental specialties, and battle formulas remain design choices.
