# Eight independently assignable gym packages

October 16, 2026. Contributor-ready **draft contracts**, not assigned collaborators,
completed gyms or approved teams. Each assignment is optional for a contributor;
all eight badges remain mandatory in the proposed campaign. Existing company/leader
casting is retained. Public-person dialogue and events are fictional.

## Shared contract

A contributor owns one gym's contained puzzle, employee-trainer scenes, leader battle,
original dialogue and asset/audio brief. The main story owns entry conditions,
badge order, recovery contribution and exit. A gym cannot add a new main villain,
require a unique legendary, alter another contributor's flags or move its office
outside the research-supported district. Request an editorial change instead.

Each package is 2–3 compact rooms plus a lobby, 2–3 optional employee battles and
one leader. Target 10–18 minutes on a first visit, subject to playtest. Show a
readiness prompt, allow leaving, provide a nearby heal/storage service, and preserve
puzzle completion through a battle loss. No real accounts, purchases, external
links or web-only input are required. Both platforms share the same GBA content.

Level/roster envelopes are proposals for the balance editor. Supply actual species,
moves, items and three obtainable counterteams once the 150-entry catalog and
learnsets are finalized. Do not substitute three verbal strategies for that later
playable balance proof. Employee identities are researched cast slots until a
contributor proposes real people; the brief does not invent actual employees.

## What to deliver

1. A three-beat scene and full compact dialogue: arrival, explanation, mistake,
   completion, leader readiness, defeat/win, reward-pending and revisit.
2. A small annotated map with entrances, exits, collision intent, puzzle stations,
   readable walk lanes and a nearby service connection.
3. Puzzle state transitions and reset/retry behavior. No one-time action may strand
   an unclaimed badge or delete a required story clue.
4. Employee and leader teams, strategy telegraphs, ordinary obtainable counters,
   a loss/retry route and a rematch proposal within the established level envelope.
5. Original visual/audio briefs and provenance; researched public-person themes.
   Real tweets, commercial sprites and commercial recordings are not new content.
6. A handoff note naming the exact input and output event IDs below. Stable semantic
   flag names are proposals until the integrator reserves native save fields.

## Contributor acceptance and integration

A story editor checks causality, public-person fiction and tone. A balance editor
checks actual obtainable teams and loss recovery. A technical integrator checks
native assets, scripts, save identifiers and both platform paths. The user reviews
quality; a contributor's submission does not close a full game acceptance gate.

A good first offer is one contained package with its current input/output contract,
not 'build anything Pokémon-like.' Source credits and license/asset provenance
travel with the package. No contributor has been contacted or promised ownership,
payment, a deadline or public association by this document.
