# Real-person cast research

Research date: October 7, 2026.
Status: first-pass public-persona research and proposed fictional casting.

## Direction

The user wants real SF tech people and recognizable online personalities for
gym leaders, villains, rivals, and ideally every interactable human NPC.
This replaces the earlier fictional-handle casting direction.

Cast across builders, AI research, investors, rationalists, accelerationists,
writers, and SF-adjacent online communities. Do not treat all candidates as
current SF residents. Locations below are invented game placements.

Public work and self-published interests inform the comedy. All battles,
dialogue, factions, and fantastical schemes below are fictional proposals.
Do not present invented actions or private-life details as biographical facts.
The player's drunk/high states do not imply substance use by the cast.

## Research limitations and next pass

Direct X profile fetches for the user-specified accounts failed. Jonathan's
own site confirms the requested handle. Independent coverage identifies
Tibo as Thibault Sottiaux; the earlier Thomas identification is not supported.

This pass confirms identities and public themes. It does not yet establish a
complete meme chronology, authentic catchphrase list, or publish-ready dialogue.
Do not copy meme-site biographies or assume similarly named profiles match.
Keep pseudonymous characters under their public names without deanonymizing them.

For each character selected for implementation, collect:

1. Canonical public name/handle and identity source.
2. SF/Bay connection, visitor status, or explicit fictional placement.
3. Three public posts, essays, or interviews supporting the intended persona.
4. Original dialogue and a distinct battle or quest mechanic.
5. Recurring appearances, callbacks, and story flags.
6. Portrait/asset provenance and release review status.

Expand toward roughly 60–80 real-person NPC identities only as neighborhood
scripts require them. Recurring characters can fill several locations.
Avoid filling the city with identical founder jokes: make each interaction
reveal a person, deliver a clue, change a quest, or provide a useful service.
