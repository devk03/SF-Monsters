mgba.sdk.js is modified to expose battery-save export/import. The modification
and reproducible patch are in scripts/prepare_web.py in the public repository:
https://github.com/devk03/SF-Monsters

The 0.10.5-compatible shim is in emulator/mgba_0105_shim.c. Its upstream
source is wasm-gaming/mGBA-wasm revision 6b19a50a1aa45055970b46999d5cde2451f1f5d0.
SF adaptations provide the older core API and a bounded raw-audio queue.
Rebuild with scripts/build_web_core.py; core-build.json records the source,
immutable Emscripten image and artifact hashes. This avoids substituting the
SDK package's different emulator revision during a clean website build.
